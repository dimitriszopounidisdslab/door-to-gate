The Concept

The airport journey begins before the airport.

Traditional airport departures require passengers to carry their checked baggage to the terminal, queue at check-in or baggage-drop counters and complete several processes before moving through security.


Door-to-Gate Check-in introduces a different model. Checked baggage can be collected from a hotel, Airbnb or designated city location before the passenger travels to the airport.


The baggage is securely transported and delivered to the airport handling process, allowing the passenger to arrive lighter and continue towards security and the departure gate.

01

Passenger convenience

Less baggage handling, fewer queues and greater freedom before departure.


02

Airport efficiency

Moving part of the baggage process outside the terminal can reduce pressure on traditional check-in infrastructure.

How It Works

From pickup to departure.

A simple passenger-facing process supported by secure baggage collection, tracking and airport integration.

01

Book

The passenger books through the app or website, provides flight details, number of bags and the preferred pickup point.

02

Bag Pickup

A Door-to-Gate representative collects the baggage, verifies the information and activates digital tracking.

03

Secure Transport

Bags are sealed, documented and securely transported to the airport within the required operational window.

04

Airport Handover

The baggage is delivered to the authorised airport handling partner and enters the flight baggage process.

05

Travel Light

The passenger arrives at the airport without checked baggage and proceeds through the remaining departure formalities.

06

Collect at Destination

The passenger collects the checked baggage normally at the destination airport.
